# Marketing Glossary - Từ Điển Thuật Ngữ Marketing

## Giới thiệu

Tài liệu này cung cấp danh sách đầy đủ các thuật ngữ chuyên ngành Marketing, bao gồm digital marketing, content marketing, social media marketing, và các công cụ liên quan. Mỗi thuật ngữ được định nghĩa chi tiết với ngữ cảnh ứng dụng trong thực tế và cách implement trong hệ thống.

## Các thuật ngữ cơ bản

### 1. Marketing Automation (Tự Động Hóa Marketing)

Marketing Automation là việc sử dụng software để automate repetitive marketing tasks, nurture prospects, và personalize customer experiences at scale. Marketing automation platforms cho phép marketers create workflows dựa trên triggers và actions, gửi targeted emails, score leads, và measure ROI của marketing activities. Các tasks có thể automate bao gồm: email marketing, social media posting, lead nurturing, scoring, và reporting.

Trong hệ thống, Marketing Automation được implement như một module với: WorkflowBuilder (visual workflow creation), TriggerEngine (event-based triggers), ActionExecutor (email, SMS, notifications), LeadScoringEngine (behavior-based scoring), và AnalyticsEngine (campaign measurement).

### 2. Lead Nurturing (Nuôi Dưỡng Lead)

Lead Nurturing là quá trình xây dựng relationships với prospects through meaningful interactions, providing relevant information, và guiding them through the sales funnel. Effective nurturing addresses prospect concerns, answers questions, và builds trust over time. Lead nurturing campaigns thường use multiple channels: email, content, social media, và personalized communications.

Lead nurturing strategy bao gồm: content mapping to buyer stages, drip campaigns, behavioral triggers, personalization, và multi-channel approach. Lead nurturing metrics: engagement rate, conversion rate, time to conversion, và pipeline velocity.

### 3. Lead Scoring (Chấm Điểm Lead)

Lead Scoring là phương pháp gán điểm cho leads dựa trên demographics, firmographics, và behavioral data để prioritize sales efforts. Explicit scoring dựa trên information provided (job title, company size, budget). Implicit scoring dựa trên behaviors (website visits, email opens, content downloads). Combined score helps identify hot leads ready for sales.

Trong hệ thống, Lead Scoring được implement với: ScoringRules (demographic + behavioral rules), WeightSystem (configurable weights), RealTimeUpdates (automatic score updates), và SalesIntegration (score-based routing).

### 4. Conversion Rate Optimization (CRO)

CRO là quá trình improving percentage of visitors who complete desired actions trên website hoặc landing page. CRO involves understanding user behavior, testing hypotheses, và implementing changes to increase conversions. Common conversion actions: form submissions, purchases, sign-ups, downloads. CRO process: analyze, hypothesize, test, iterate.

A/B testing là core technique trong CRO. Testing elements: headlines, CTAs, images, forms, layout. Statistical significance cần đạt before declaring winner. Mobile optimization increasingly important cho CRO.

### 5. Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)

CAC là tổng chi phí để có được một khách hàng mới, bao gồm tất cả chi phí marketing và sales. CAC calculation: (Total Marketing + Sales Costs) / Number of New Customers. CAC by channel helps identify most cost-effective acquisition channels. CAC compared với Customer Lifetime Value (CLV) determines profitability.

Strategies to reduce CAC: improve conversion rates, optimize marketing channels, increase retention, automate processes. CAC trends over time indicate marketing efficiency.

### 6. Customer Lifetime Value (CLV)

CLV là tổng doanh thu mà một khách hàng mang lại trong suốt vòng đời của họ. CLV calculation: (Average Purchase Value × Purchase Frequency × Customer Lifespan) - Acquisition Cost. CLV by segment helps identify most valuable customer groups. CLV:CAC ratio indicates business sustainability (healthy ratio > 3:1).

CLV prediction models use historical data và machine learning để forecast future customer value. CLV optimization involves balancing acquisition costs, retention investments, và upselling opportunities.

### 7. Email Marketing

Email Marketing là việc sử dụng email để promote products, nurture leads, và build relationships với customers. Email marketing remains one of most effective marketing channels với high ROI. Types of email: promotional, transactional, lifecycle, triggered. Email marketing KPIs: open rate, click-through rate, conversion rate, unsubscribe rate, ROI.

Email marketing automation enables: welcome sequences, abandoned cart emails, re-engagement campaigns, birthday emails, reorders. Personalization và segmentation improve email performance significantly.

### 8. Content Marketing

Content Marketing là việc tạo và distribute valuable, relevant content để attract và engage target audience. Content marketing strategy bao gồm: content planning, creation, optimization, distribution, và measurement. Content types: blog posts, videos, infographics, podcasts, ebooks, webinars, case studies.

Content marketing goals: brand awareness, lead generation, customer engagement, thought leadership. Content calendar helps plan và organize content creation. SEO integration essential for content discoverability.

### 9. Social Media Marketing

Social Media Marketing là việc sử dụng social platforms để connect với audience, build brand, increase sales, và drive traffic. Social platforms: Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, Twitter, TikTok, Pinterest. Each platform có unique audience, content format, và engagement style.

Social media strategy bao gồm: platform selection, content strategy, posting schedule, community management, paid advertising. Social media metrics: reach, engagement, clicks, conversions, sentiment. Social listening tools monitor brand mentions và industry conversations.

### 10. Search Engine Optimization (SEO)

SEO là practice of optimizing website để improve visibility trong search engine results pages (SERPs). SEO includes: on-page optimization (content, structure, HTML), off-page optimization (backlinks, social signals), technical SEO (site speed, mobile, crawlability). Goal: rank higher for relevant keywords.

SEO process: keyword research, content optimization, technical improvements, link building, monitoring, iteration. SEO metrics: organic traffic, keyword rankings, backlink profile, domain authority. Algorithm updates require ongoing adaptation.

### 11. Pay-Per-Click Advertising (PPC)

PPC là advertising model where advertisers pay each time someone clicks on their ad. PPC platforms: Google Ads, Bing Ads, social media ads. PPC campaign types: search ads, display ads, shopping ads, video ads. PPC offers precise targeting và immediate traffic.

PPC management: keyword research, ad creation, bidding strategy, landing page optimization, campaign monitoring, optimization. PPC metrics: impressions, clicks, CTR, CPC, conversions, CPA, ROAS.

### 12. Marketing Qualified Lead (MQL)

MQL là lead that has engaged with marketing content và meets certain criteria indicating readiness for sales follow-up. MQL definition varies by organization: website engagement, content downloads, email interactions, demo requests. MQL criteria được defined collaboratively by marketing và sales teams.

MQL vs Sales Qualified Lead (SQL): MQL indicates marketing-ready, SQL indicates sales-ready. Lead routing: MQLs sent to sales with context, SQLs scheduled for sales calls. MQL to SQL conversion rate measures marketing effectiveness.

### 13. Return on Investment (ROI)

ROI là metric measuring profitability của marketing investments. Marketing ROI calculation: (Revenue from Marketing - Marketing Cost) / Marketing Cost. ROI helps justify marketing spend và optimize resource allocation. Different attribution models affect ROI calculations.

Marketing ROI by channel: compare performance across email, social, paid ads, content. Long-term vs short-term ROI: some investments (brand building) take time to show returns. Target ROI varies by industry và business model.

### 14. Attribution Model (Mô Hình Phân Bổ)

Attribution Model là rules determining how credit for conversions is assigned to different touchpoints in customer journey. Attribution models: First-touch (100% credit to first interaction), Last-touch (100% credit to last interaction), Linear (equal credit to all touchpoints), Time-decay (more credit to recent touchpoints), Multi-touch (complex algorithms).

Attribution helps understand customer journey và optimize marketing spend. Multi-touch attribution increasingly important as customer journeys become more complex. Data availability affects model accuracy.

### 15. Funnel (Phễu Marketing)

Marketing Funnel là visual representation of customer journey từ awareness đến conversion. Funnel stages: Awareness (top), Consideration (middle), Decision (bottom). Each stage requires different marketing approach và content. Funnel metrics track progression through stages.

Full-funnel marketing addresses all stages: ATL (above-the-line) for awareness, BTL (below-the-line) for consideration và conversion. Funnel analysis identifies bottlenecks và optimization opportunities.

### 16. Landing Page

Landing Page là standalone web page created specifically for marketing campaign. Landing pages focus on single objective: conversion. Elements: compelling headline, value proposition, benefits, social proof, CTA, minimal navigation. Landing page optimization essential for campaign success.

Landing page best practices: clear headline, benefit-focused copy, visual hierarchy, trust signals, mobile-responsive, fast loading. A/B testing landing pages improves conversion rates over time.

### 17. Call to Action (CTA)

CTA là prompt encouraging audience to take specific action: "Sign Up", "Download", "Buy Now", "Learn More". Effective CTAs are: clear, action-oriented, benefit-driven, visually prominent. CTA placement: above fold, after value proposition, at end of content.

CTA optimization: color, size, text, placement. Testing different CTAs helps improve click-through rates. CTAs should align with landing page offer.

### 18. Bounce Rate

Bounce Rate là percentage of visitors who leave website after viewing only one page. High bounce rate indicates: irrelevant traffic, poor user experience, slow loading, unclear navigation. Bounce rate by page helps identify underperforming content.

Bounce rate reduction strategies: improve page speed, enhance content relevance, optimize navigation, internal linking, clear CTAs. Industry benchmarks vary: e-commerce (30-55%), B2B (25-55%), landing pages (70-90%).

### 19. Click-Through Rate (CTR)

CTR là ratio of users who click on link to number of total users who view page, email, or ad. CTR calculation: (Clicks / Impressions) × 100. CTR measures effectiveness của marketing creative và targeting. Higher CTR generally indicates more relevant messaging.

CTR benchmarks: email (2-5%), Google Ads (2-5% search, 0.5-1% display), social media (0.5-1%). CTR improvement: compelling copy, relevant images, clear value proposition, testing.

### 20. Customer Retention

Customer Retention là ability of company to retain customers over time. Retention metrics: retention rate, churn rate, repeat purchase rate, NPS. Retention strategies: loyalty programs, personalized experiences, excellent customer service, regular engagement.

Retention vs Acquisition: increasing retention 5% can increase profits 25-95%. Retention cost much lower than acquisition. Customer success teams focus on maximizing retention.

## Kết luận

Từ điển thuật ngữ này cung cấp nền tảng kiến thức vững chắc về các khái niệm Marketing. Việc hiểu rõ từng thuật ngữ giúp xây dựng hệ thống Marketing hiệu quả và phù hợp với nhu cầu kinh doanh.
