# Billing Knowledge - Glossary

## Core Concepts
- **Subscription**: Recurring payment arrangement
- **Plan/Tier**: Product offering with defined features and limits
- **Invoice**: Bill document sent to customer
- **Payment method**: Card, bank transfer, e-wallet
- **Transaction**: Individual payment event
- **Refund**: Return of previously paid amount
- **Dunning**: Process of collecting overdue payments

## Subscription Models
- **Per-seat**: Per user pricing (active users counted monthly)
- **Flat fee**: Fixed price per tenant/month
- **Usage-based**: Variable pricing based on consumption
- **Tiered**: Volume-based pricing with breaks
- **Hybrid**: Base fee + usage overage

## Payment Providers (Vietnam)
- **MoMo**: E-wallet, QR payment, MoMo API integration
- **SePay**: Banking API, auto-reconciliation
- **PayOS**: Card and banking QR via VietQR
- **ZaloPay**: Zalo e-wallet integration
- **VNPay**: Gateway with many payment methods
- **VietQR**: Bank QR standardization

## Subscription States
- **Trial**: Free period before billing starts
- **Active**: Paying customer
- **Past due**: Payment failed, grace period
- **Suspended**: Service limited, payment overdue
- **Cancelled**: Subscription ended
- **Churned**: Voluntary or involuntary cancellation

## Billing Events
- **Subscription created**: First plan assignment
- **Subscription renewed**: Recurring billing cycle
- **Plan changed**: Upgrade/downgrade
- **Payment succeeded**: Successful charge
- **Payment failed**: Failed charge attempt
- **Refund processed**: Money returned to customer
- **Credit applied**: Account credit used

## Pricing Strategy Terms
- **ARR (Annual Recurring Revenue)**: Annualized subscription revenue
- **MRR (Monthly Recurring Revenue)**: Monthly subscription revenue
- **LTV (Lifetime Value)**: Total revenue from customer over relationship
- **CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost)**: Cost to acquire customer
- **Churn rate**: Percentage of customers cancelling
- **NRR (Net Revenue Retention)**: Expansion + contraction + churn
